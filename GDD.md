# GDD — Burbank Walkabout

## Fantasy

You are on foot in downtown Burbank. Streets and building outlines follow the real downtown: Olive, Magnolia, San Fernando, Glenoaks, and the numbered streets. City Hall is at 275 East Olive, Town Center is the mall on Magnolia, and the central library is on Glenoaks.

## Core loop

1. Walk the sidewalks and streets.
2. Meet a person who is already out walking.
3. Talk, by voice or by typing. They answer in a short spoken line.
4. Other players who join the same server share the block and can hear nearby lines.

## Multiplayer

One street server owns where the pedestrians are, so every client sees the same walk. Your position is sent as text. Conversation with a person is text in, text out. Speech recognition and speech synthesis never leave the browser.

## Non-goals

- The full city, parcels, or live map tiles
- Accounts, persistence, or combat
- Streaming microphone audio to the server
- Wardom's combat, camps, or character exports
